# Privacy Policy for Ed Reply Time Splitter

**Last updated: October 1, 2026**

Ed Reply Time Splitter is a Chrome extension designed to help users analyze participation in an Ed discussion thread by identifying responders and grouping them according to whether they replied before or after a user-selected cutoff time.

## Data the Extension Processes

The extension processes information that is already visible to the signed-in user on the currently open Ed discussion page. This may include:

* Names of users who responded to the discussion
* Reply dates and timestamps
* Other webpage content necessary to associate a responder with a reply timestamp

The extension only accesses the active browser tab when the user chooses to use the extension.

## How the Data Is Used

This information is used solely to provide the extension's functionality, including:

* Identifying people who responded to an Ed discussion
* Determining whether they responded before or after a selected cutoff time
* Displaying the resulting lists to the user
* Allowing the user to copy the resulting names to their clipboard

## Data Storage

The extension does not send responder names, timestamps, discussion content, or other Ed data to an external server.

Processing occurs locally within the user's browser.

The extension does not maintain a remote database and does not permanently store the analyzed Ed discussion data. Results exist only as necessary for the extension to provide its functionality. If a user copies a list to their clipboard, that copied information is subsequently controlled by the user's browser and operating system.

## Data Sharing

The extension does not sell, rent, transfer, or share user data with third parties.

The developer does not receive or have access to the names, timestamps, discussion content, or results processed by the extension.

## Analytics and Advertising

The extension does not use analytics services, advertising services, tracking technologies, or behavioral profiling.

## Security

Because the extension processes Ed discussion information locally and does not transmit that information to a developer-operated server, the extension is designed to minimize unnecessary transfer or retention of user data.

## Chrome Web Store Limited Use

The extension's use of user data is limited to providing its stated user-facing functionality and complies with the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

## Changes to This Privacy Policy

This privacy policy may be updated if the functionality or data practices of the extension change. Any updated policy will be posted at this location.
